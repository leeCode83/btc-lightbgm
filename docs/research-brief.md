# Research brief: I want to build a machine learning regression model that predict

**Question:** Research brief: I want to build a machine learning regression model that predicts the price of Bitcoin 5 hours ahead. I need evidence-based guidance on four things:

1. ALGORITHMS: Which ML/DL architectures have actually been validated in peer-reviewed studies for short-horizon (1-24 hour) Bitcoin price prediction? Specifically compare: (a) gradient boosting trees (XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost) on engineered features vs (b) deep sequence models (LSTM, GRU, CNN-LSTM, Temporal Convolutional Networks, Transformers/Informer/PatchTST) vs (c) classical econometric/statistical baselines (ARIMA, GARCH, random walk). What is the reported predictive performance (RMSE, MAE, MAPE, directional accuracy, R-squared) and — critically — do these models beat a naive random-walk / last-price baseline out-of-sample once transaction costs and realistic evaluation are applied? Report effect sizes and note studies that found NO significant edge.

2. TARGET DESIGN: Evidence on predicting raw price level vs log returns vs volatility vs classification of direction (up/down/flat) for short horizons. Which target formulation produces models that generalize out-of-sample? Any evidence on multi-step / direct vs recursive forecasting for a 5-hour horizon?

3. DATA GRANULARITY AND HISTORY LENGTH: What sampling frequency (1-minute, 5-minute, 15-minute, 1-hour candles) and how many years of history are used in studies that report credible out-of-sample results? Evidence on how much history is needed to cover multiple market regimes (bull/bear/sideways), and on data leakage from overlapping windows when the forecast horizon (5h) is much longer than the sampling interval (1m). Any guidance on purged/embargoed cross-validation or walk-forward validation for overlapping-horizon financial ML.

4. FEATURES / EXOGENOUS DATA: Which feature families have shown incremental predictive value for BTC at hourly scale: technical indicators (RSI, MACD, Bollinger), realized volatility, order-book depth/imbalance, funding rates, open interest, on-chain metrics, sentiment (news/Twitter/Fear&Greed), and cross-asset (S&P500, gold, DXY, stablecoin flows)? Which ones survive out-of-sample and which are spurious?

Include seminal and recent (2020-2026) references with DOIs where possible. Prioritize studies with rigorous out-of-sample/walk-forward evaluation over studies reporting only in-sample fit. Also flag the base-rate / efficient-market caveat: what hit rate or R-squared is realistically achievable for a 5-hour BTC horizon, and what magnitude of edge do the best papers claim after costs?

# **Bitcoin 5-Hour Price Prediction**: What the Evidence Supports

The literature validates short-horizon BTC forecasting only weakly: models beat random classification but rarely beat a last-price baseline or transaction costs out-of-sample. Below, the four facets — algorithms, target design, data/validation, and features — with the base-rate caveat threaded through.

## Algorithm Families and Their Reported Performance

| Evidence Strength | Claim |
|---|---|
| Evidence strength: Moderate (7/10) | **Gradient boosting and recurrent nets** lead on directional accuracy, but only modestly: 50.9–56.0% binary accuracy across 1–60 min horizons, with XGBoost best for BTC at 55.9% [[1.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/jaquart-dann-shortterm-bitcoin-market-prediction-via-machine/w:b3d1bfc8-7a60-4ceb-9576-20a6a10f5c32/) [[2.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/hasanli-dursun-integrating-highdimensional-technical-indicators/w:f6edded5-09bf-472a-b64e-4b4ad5892135/) [[2.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/hasanli-dursun-integrating-highdimensional-technical-indicators/w:f6edded5-09bf-472a-b64e-4b4ad5892135/). |
| Evidence strength: Moderate (6/10) | **GRU/LSTM** win on regression error in several benchmarks (GRU test R²=0.9138, MAPE 3.84%; LSTM best average RMSE 0.0222), yet these are price-level fits, not edge over a naive baseline [[3.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/ngo-chi-benchmarking-deep-learning-models-for-bitcoin/w:26d235f2-4a6b-4a9f-b396-5814de94b992/) [[4.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/murray-rossi-on-forecasting-cryptocurrency-prices-a-comparison/w:df3774b8-2ab5-4f3a-881f-597fd17cfb05/) [[5.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/dutta-kumar-a-gated-recurrent-unit-approach-to-bitcoin-price/w:955253e7-fbb4-4a58-ad71-2986edf58f85/). |
| Evidence strength: Weak (3/10) | **No after-cost edge**: quantile long-short strategies yield up to 116% before costs but negative returns after, and ROC-AUC ≈0.60 models cannot generate economically significant returns net of fees [[1.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/jaquart-dann-shortterm-bitcoin-market-prediction-via-machine/w:b3d1bfc8-7a60-4ceb-9576-20a6a10f5c32/) [[1.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/jaquart-dann-shortterm-bitcoin-market-prediction-via-machine/w:b3d1bfc8-7a60-4ceb-9576-20a6a10f5c32/) [[6.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/sobreiro-martinho-multitimeframe-feature-engineering-for-bitcoin/w:db81b812-3361-4c5a-9e40-2bdce58287a3/). |

Deep sequence models are the most-benchmarked family, but the ranking is unstable: GRU beats Transformer, LSTM, TCN, CNN and vanilla RNN on daily BTC (R²=0.9138 vs 0.4942 for Transformer), while CNN/TCN/RNN show negative test R² [[3.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/ngo-chi-benchmarking-deep-learning-models-for-bitcoin/w:26d235f2-4a6b-4a9f-b396-5814de94b992/) [[3.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/ngo-chi-benchmarking-deep-learning-models-for-bitcoin/w:26d235f2-4a6b-4a9f-b396-5814de94b992/). Bi-LSTM with a short window outperformed SOTA iTransformer and TSMixer [[7.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/jung-kim-decoding-bitcoin-leveraging-macro-and-microfactors/w:8096221e-2616-43ab-baf9-d210e7e9e022/), and CNN-LSTM with Boruta feature selection reached 82.44% directional accuracy [[8.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/omole-enke-deep-learning-for-bitcoin-price-direction/w:0ec2736b-1712-4430-a72c-f7b8ddaea81d/) — though a companion study reports 82.03% for the same combination [[9.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/dubey-enke-bitcoin-price-direction-prediction-using-onchain/w:5bf07d0f-a934-4234-8942-268f72b483aa/), and such figures sit far above the 50–56% range other studies report, a red flag for leakage or regime-specific luck.

Classical baselines are consistently beaten on error metrics but rarely on economics. ARIMA underperforms ML methods in multiple comparisons [[10.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/chen-analysis-of-bitcoin-price-prediction-using-machine/w:0f0934f8-b2a6-4996-943b-6481302180a3/), and RNN/LSTM beat ARIMA in early work [[11.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/ji-kim-a-comparative-study-of-bitcoin-price-prediction/w:bb1274a8-417f-4849-ad9f-e029a4ce8ddb/). Yet the decisive test is the random-walk/last-price benchmark: Jaquart et al. find statistically viable predictions that still fail to cover transaction costs [[1.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/jaquart-dann-shortterm-bitcoin-market-prediction-via-machine/w:b3d1bfc8-7a60-4ceb-9576-20a6a10f5c32/) [[1.4]](https://consensus.app/papers/jaquart-dann-shortterm-bitcoin-market-prediction-via-machine/w:b3d1bfc8-7a60-4ceb-9576-20a6a10f5c32/), and a 2026 study concludes ROC-AUC ≈0.60 is insufficient for net profitability [[6.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/sobreiro-martinho-multitimeframe-feature-engineering-for-bitcoin/w:db81b812-3361-4c5a-9e40-2bdce58287a3/).

## Target Design: Price Level vs Returns vs Direction

Predicting raw price level inflates apparent accuracy because the series is non-stationary and trending; high R² values (0.98–0.99) in level-prediction studies reflect persistence, not skill [[12.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/anwas-fahrianto-performance-comparison-of-lstm-xgboost-and/w:fa91292b-afa0-4941-9fa5-ea7e26804567/) [[7.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/jung-kim-decoding-bitcoin-leveraging-macro-and-microfactors/w:8096221e-2616-43ab-baf9-d210e7e9e022/). Direction classification is harder but more honest: classification models scored up to 65% next-day accuracy while the authors note it is "much harder to forecast its rise and fall" [[13.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/mudassir-bennbaia-timeseries-forecasting-of-bitcoin-prices-using/w:4385f101-652e-49ae-b649-41216ea2f2b7/) [[13.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/mudassir-bennbaia-timeseries-forecasting-of-bitcoin-prices-using/w:4385f101-652e-49ae-b649-41216ea2f2b7/). Log-return targets are used in multivariate settings where multivariate LSTM beat univariate methods on error [[14.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/miller-kim-univariate-and-multivariate-machine-learning/w:3070e090-e32c-4b0d-8a27-7040b7eae790/) [[14.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/miller-kim-univariate-and-multivariate-machine-learning/w:3070e090-e32c-4b0d-8a27-7040b7eae790/). For a 5-hour horizon, direct one-step-ahead setups with strict chronological splits are the documented protocol [[12.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/anwas-fahrianto-performance-comparison-of-lstm-xgboost-and/w:fa91292b-afa0-4941-9fa5-ea7e26804567/) [[12.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/anwas-fahrianto-performance-comparison-of-lstm-xgboost-and/w:fa91292b-afa0-4941-9fa5-ea7e26804567/); classification targets proved more effective than regression for algorithmic trading [[11.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/ji-kim-a-comparative-study-of-bitcoin-price-prediction/w:bb1274a8-417f-4849-ad9f-e029a4ce8ddb/).

## Data Granularity, History, and Leakage Control

Studies reporting credible out-of-sample results span 1-minute to daily bars: 9 months of minutely data [[1.5]](https://consensus.app/papers/jaquart-dann-shortterm-bitcoin-market-prediction-via-machine/w:b3d1bfc8-7a60-4ceb-9576-20a6a10f5c32/), 15-minute intervals over ~18 months [[15.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/forecasting-bitcoin-price-direction-with-random/w:3cb1bcf5-897e-418e-beaa-e199036642df/), 15-minute data with 2020–2025 coverage [[6.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/sobreiro-martinho-multitimeframe-feature-engineering-for-bitcoin/w:db81b812-3361-4c5a-9e40-2bdce58287a3/), and 2,283 daily observations across 2020–2026 [[3.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/ngo-chi-benchmarking-deep-learning-models-for-bitcoin/w:26d235f2-4a6b-4a9f-b396-5814de94b992/). Longer single samples rarely exceed 4 years in earlier work [[10.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/chen-analysis-of-bitcoin-price-prediction-using-machine/w:0f0934f8-b2a6-4996-943b-6481302180a3/), and one study used 2,590 days [[11.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/ji-kim-a-comparative-study-of-bitcoin-price-prediction/w:bb1274a8-417f-4849-ad9f-e029a4ce8ddb/).

Leakage is the dominant threat with overlapping windows. A conservation law shows strictly causal schemes satisfy α+β≤1, and purged k-fold with an embargo works because leakage harm is governed by severity, not volume [[16.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/li-the-impossible-trinity-of-timeseries-validation-a/w:e4d5f6c4-0bfa-4467-85fa-e8abc3ab2a03/) [[16.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/li-the-impossible-trinity-of-timeseries-validation-a/w:e4d5f6c4-0bfa-4467-85fa-e8abc3ab2a03/). Shuffled 5-fold CV reported an information coefficient of +0.32 on pure noise [[16.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/li-the-impossible-trinity-of-timeseries-validation-a/w:e4d5f6c4-0bfa-4467-85fa-e8abc3ab2a03/), and a multi-timeframe look-ahead bias inflated ROC-AUC by ~0.20 points before correction [[6.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/sobreiro-martinho-multitimeframe-feature-engineering-for-bitcoin/w:db81b812-3361-4c5a-9e40-2bdce58287a3/). Centered temporal features and same-day-open execution cause large, stable inflation in backtests [[17.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/zhang-li-when-alpha-disappears-a-oneswitch-benchmark-for/w:b9b560d9-7b53-4b7e-a607-2f448561e41f/).

## Feature Families: What Survives Out-of-Sample

Technical indicators are the most consistently important feature family across methods [[1.6]](https://consensus.app/papers/jaquart-dann-shortterm-bitcoin-market-prediction-via-machine/w:b3d1bfc8-7a60-4ceb-9576-20a6a10f5c32/) [[1.7]](https://consensus.app/papers/jaquart-dann-shortterm-bitcoin-market-prediction-via-machine/w:b3d1bfc8-7a60-4ceb-9576-20a6a10f5c32/) [[15.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/forecasting-bitcoin-price-direction-with-random/w:3cb1bcf5-897e-418e-beaa-e199036642df/), with 4-hour Bollinger position and RSI dominant in a multi-timeframe pipeline [[6.4]](https://consensus.app/papers/sobreiro-martinho-multitimeframe-feature-engineering-for-bitcoin/w:db81b812-3361-4c5a-9e40-2bdce58287a3/). On-chain features add value, particularly realized/unrealized value categories [[9.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/dubey-enke-bitcoin-price-direction-prediction-using-onchain/w:5bf07d0f-a934-4234-8942-268f72b483aa/), and combining sentiment, technical, on-chain, traditional-asset and macro data beat price-only models in ablation [[7.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/jung-kim-decoding-bitcoin-leveraging-macro-and-microfactors/w:8096221e-2616-43ab-baf9-d210e7e9e022/). Sentiment and social indicators raised daily classification from 51–55% to 67–84% in one study [[18.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/ortu-uras-on-technical-trading-and-social-media-indicators/w:529e97f3-4996-46e8-bb55-8e333c6a94b6/), though that jump is unusually large and not replicated elsewhere in this corpus.

Cross-asset signals are regime-dependent: US equity indices, oil and ETH mattered 2015–2018, shifting to ETH and JP225 after 2018 [[10.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/chen-analysis-of-bitcoin-price-prediction-using-machine/w:0f0934f8-b2a6-4996-943b-6481302180a3/) [[10.4]](https://consensus.app/papers/chen-analysis-of-bitcoin-price-prediction-using-machine/w:0f0934f8-b2a6-4996-943b-6481302180a3/), and oil-price volatility mattered for both BTC and gold [[15.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/forecasting-bitcoin-price-direction-with-random/w:3cb1bcf5-897e-418e-beaa-e199036642df/). Macro variables like interest rates and inflation are important but their importance changes over time [[15.4]](https://consensus.app/papers/forecasting-bitcoin-price-direction-with-random/w:3cb1bcf5-897e-418e-beaa-e199036642df/) [[15.5]](https://consensus.app/papers/forecasting-bitcoin-price-direction-with-random/w:3cb1bcf5-897e-418e-beaa-e199036642df/).

## The Base-Rate Reality for a 5-Hour Horizon

Realistically, expect **50–56% directional accuracy** and **near-zero net edge after costs** [[1.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/jaquart-dann-shortterm-bitcoin-market-prediction-via-machine/w:b3d1bfc8-7a60-4ceb-9576-20a6a10f5c32/) [[2.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/hasanli-dursun-integrating-highdimensional-technical-indicators/w:f6edded5-09bf-472a-b64e-4b4ad5892135/) [[3.4]](https://consensus.app/papers/ngo-chi-benchmarking-deep-learning-models-for-bitcoin/w:26d235f2-4a6b-4a9f-b396-5814de94b992/). The near-50% directional accuracy of all models confirms the near-efficient nature of BTC direction [[3.4]](https://consensus.app/papers/ngo-chi-benchmarking-deep-learning-models-for-bitcoin/w:26d235f2-4a6b-4a9f-b396-5814de94b992/), and the best papers claim returns that vanish once fees are applied [[1.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/jaquart-dann-shortterm-bitcoin-market-prediction-via-machine/w:b3d1bfc8-7a60-4ceb-9576-20a6a10f5c32/) [[6.5]](https://consensus.app/papers/sobreiro-martinho-multitimeframe-feature-engineering-for-bitcoin/w:db81b812-3361-4c5a-9e40-2bdce58287a3/). Build with purged/embargoed walk-forward validation, a last-price baseline, and cost-aware evaluation — and treat any R² above ~0.9 on price levels as persistence, not predictive skill.
